## Tables (own, migration `226_tax_model`)
- `tax226_product_profiles` (tenant, product → FK `products`; version): `category` standard | zw | np | oo; ZW: `exemption_basis_kind` P_19A/P_19B/P_19C + `exemption_basis` (1–256, FA(3) P_19A/B/C); NP: `np_kind` np_i (FA(3) `np I`: poza krajem, bez art. 100 ust. 1 pkt 4 i OSS) | np_ii (`np II`: usługi z art. 100 ust. 1 pkt 4, art. 28b, dla podatnika z UE); `gtu` GTU_01–13 or null; `annex15` (MPP goods/services); `oss_rate_class`. No row = ordinary rate from `products.vat`.
- `tax226_oss_settings` (tenant: registered, registered_from) + `tax226_oss_rates` (tenant, EU-26 country, rate class, rate 0–30).
- `tax226_document_snapshots` (tenant, document → FK `documents`): buyer kind/country, procedure domestic|oss, gross, annex-15 gross, `mpp_required`, actor/key, `captured_with_issue`; `tax226_document_lines` (line_no = position in `documents.data.lines`): category, rate, FA(3) rate code (23/8/5/0 KR/0 WDT/0 EX/oss/zw/np I/np II/oo), basis, NP kind, GTU, annex 15, profile version. Append-only (guard trigger on own tables, also for the owner).

Core tables get nothing besides the internal RI triggers of the FKs (products, documents). RLS `tenant_scope`, dyna_app grants per table. CHECKs encode the category rules.

## Rules (`server/tax226-model.ts`, pure)
- ZW needs basis kind + text; NP needs its kind; other categories refuse basis/NP fields.
- OO: buyer must be a PL active VAT payer (B2B). NP II (art. 100 ust. 1 pkt 4): B2B buyer with an EU VAT ID outside PL (reverse charge, FA(3) `P_18=1`). NP I: place of supply outside PL (refused for a PL buyer). Corrected 23.10.2026: the first version had the two meanings swapped; stored codes unchanged.
- OSS: B2C buyer in another EU state, registration valid on the sell date, a destination rate for the line's class; otherwise refused. B2B/non-EU export (WDT/EX) not yet supported.
- MPP: B2B, gross strictly over 15 000 PLN, at least one annex-15 line.

## Issue (snapshot at issue time)
`server/local-invoices.ts` is hash-pinned by `scripts/full-base-scale-http-ports.mjs`, so it is untouched. `POST /api/local-invoices/previews/:id/tax-issue` (`server/tax226-local-invoices.ts`) runs the native SQL contract in one transaction (frame, locks, `local_invoice_issue`, audit) and writes the snapshot before `SET CONSTRAINTS ALL IMMEDIATE`; the Base `addInvoice` port does the same. Facts are checked before numbering: local math (157) knows only 0/5/8/23 for a PL consumer, so ZW/NP/OO lines and MPP are refused (409) until the FA(3) step. A replay returns the snapshot of its own issue; an invoice issued via the pinned `/issue` before 24.10.2026 has no snapshot (`tax: null` + note), no late snapshot is invented. Since 24.10.2026 (gaps231) the pinned `/issue` is an alias of `tax-issue`: an app-level guard in `server/app.ts` (`local-invoice-issue-alias.ts`, registered before the pinned routes) refuses an order with a FV-S or another 157 invoice with 409 and sends every other request to the `tax-issue` handler, so the snapshot is always written; the pinned files are unchanged.
