# RECEIPTS233: fiscal receipts on a local fiscal printer (25.10.2026)

Branch `goal/full-base-20261017`, local only. Migration `233_fiscal_receipts` adds the fiscal receipt
(paragon fiskalny) of a B2C order, printed on a fiscal printer (kasa fiskalna online), as a local contract:
there is no real hardware and no real printer protocol. Commits: 09bbd2e (model), 30cd000 (printer port, driver, fake printer), 1b039e4 (SQL), 0c2ab8b (service, routes, invoice to receipt, FA(3), settlement, RODO), 6638c50 (security fix of the printer test), 575e07e (UI), 3ad5a7d (registration and packaging), d318368 (daily report fix), 03aaeba (compiled cold inverse), 9a4a70a (UI fixes from the browser check), and the DB test and this document.

## Legal frame (what the app encodes)
- A B2C sale is registered on a fiscal cash register unless exempt. The register itself sends its data to
  the Central Repository of Cash Registers (JPK_KASA); the app does not produce JPK_KASA.
- A receipt with the buyer's NIP and a total of at most 450 zł (or 100 EUR) is a simplified invoice
  (art. 106e ust. 5 pkt 3 ustawy o VAT). Receipts here are PLN only, so the limit is 450,00 zł.
- An invoice to a receipt (faktura do paragonu) names the receipt. For a business buyer it may be issued
  only when the receipt has that buyer's NIP (art. 106b ust. 5).
- The task's rule is followed: an order that already has an invoice (157, 230, B2B, Base, external plan)
  gets no receipt, and a B2B order is documented by an invoice, not a receipt.
- Not encoded (documented only): the exemptions from the cash register (e.g. the mail-order exemption for
  payments wholly through a bank, with records identifying each sale) and the 3-month time limit for an
  invoice to a receipt (art. 106b ust. 3).

## Storage (migration 233, own tables only)
- `receipt233_printers` — the printer of the tenant:
  - name, model family (`posnet` | `elzab` | `novitus`), host (loopback only: `127.0.0.1`, `::1`,
    `localhost`, CHECKed), port 1024–65535, unique number (`[A-Z]{3}[0-9]{8}`), the PTU table A–G, active;
  - at most one active printer per tenant (partial unique index);
  - the configuration changes one version at a time; the unique number and identity never change, a row
    is never deleted (its receipts point to it). Another device is a new row.
- `receipt233_receipts` — one receipt job per order:
  - the snapshot the user confirmed (`receipt233/v1`: order, printer, PTU table, lines with PTU letters,
    PTU totals, totals, payment form, buyer NIP, simplified-invoice flag), its `scope_hash`;
  - `state` `queued` → `sent` → `printed` | `failed`, `failed` → `queued` on a retry; `attempts`;
  - the printer's receipt number and fiscal date, or the error code and Polish message;
  - `client_key` (idempotency), one receipt per order, unique (unique number, fiscal date, number);
  - CHECKs: gross = net + VAT; simplified invoice ⇔ NIP and gross ≤ 45 000 gr; printed ⇔ number, date and
    time; failed ⇔ error; the snapshot agrees with the columns.
  - A BEFORE guard: a new receipt is queued on the active printer of its tenant for an order without an
    invoice; afterwards only the state and the printer's answer move, along the allowed transitions; a
    printed receipt never changes. DELETE and TRUNCATE are refused, also for the owner.
- `receipt233_daily_reports` — the daily fiscal report read from the printer (number, receipts, gross and
  VAT per PTU) with the app's sums of the receipts printed on that printer that day and `matches`.
  Append-only, one per printer and fiscal date.
- `receipt233_invoice_links` — invoice to a receipt: one invoice per receipt and one receipt per invoice.
  A BEFORE guard requires a printed receipt of the same order, the same gross amount and, for a business
  buyer, its NIP on the receipt. Append-only.
- The only foreign keys leave the own tables for `orders` and `documents` (their internal RI triggers are
  the only objects on core tables). RLS `tenant_scope`; `dyna_app` gets SELECT/INSERT plus UPDATE of the
  configuration columns of a printer and of the state columns of a receipt.
- The hash-pinned `local-invoices.ts`, `local-invoice-routes.ts` and `local-invoice-values.ts` are unchanged.

## PTU mapping (reversible choice)
| 226/230 rate code | PTU value | customary letter |
|---|---|---|
| 23 | 23% | A |
| 8 | 8% | B |
| 5 | 5% | C |
| 0 KR | 0% | D |
| zw | zwolnione | E |
| np I, np II, oo, oss | — (not registered on a Polish cash register: refused) | — |

The letter of a line is the letter the printer's configured PTU table gives its value, so a printer
programmed differently (e.g. A 8%, B 23%) is followed. F and G are unused by default. A rate the table lacks
refuses the receipt ("drukarka nie ma litery PTU dla stawki …").

## Amounts (rounding identical to the invoice math)
- Lines are the order items and the shipping line, categorised by the 226 profiles seen from a Polish
  consumer exactly as the FV-S invoice does (`invoiceMath` of 230; shipping follows the goods).
- Per line from the gross amount: VAT = gross × r / (100 + r), half up, net = gross − VAT; ZW VAT 0.
  PTU totals are sums of lines, so an invoice to the receipt has the same gross per rate.
- A cash register computes VAT on the PTU total of the receipt/day, which can differ by a grosz from the sum
  of per-line VAT (the fake printer does this; the unit test shows 19 998 gr at 23%: 3 739 on the total,
  3 740 per line). The daily report therefore compares the gross per PTU and the receipt count; the VAT of
  both is stored.
- One exemption basis per receipt is not required (no P_19 on a receipt).

## Printer port and the local protocol
- `FiscalPrinterDriver` (`server/receipt233-printer.ts`): `status()`, `query(jobId)`,
  `printReceipt(job)`, `dailyReport()`. A real driver (Posnet Thermal/XML, Elzab, Novitus) implements the
  same port; those protocols are not in scope. Every family is served by the DYNA-FISC/1 driver, which
  refuses a device that does not answer HELLO with that protocol.
- DYNA-FISC/1: TCP on a loopback host, one UTF-8 line per request and per answer:
  `HELLO`, `PTUCHECK`, `QUERY <job>`, `NIP`, `OPEN <job>`, `LINE`, `PAY`, `CLOSE <total>`, `CANCEL`,
  `DAILY`; errors `PAPER_OUT`, `PTU_MISMATCH`, `TOTAL_MISMATCH`, offline (no answer, refused or closed
  connection). The full grammar is in the module header.
- Idempotency: the job id is the receipt id. Before every print the driver asks `QUERY`; a job already
  printed is returned, never printed twice. A job left in `sent` (the app lost the answer) is recovered by
  "Sprawdź i ponów druk", which asks the printer first.
- An uncertain outcome stays `sent` (26.10.2026, S4 RES-18): when the connection drops or the printer does
  not answer after `OPEN` (LINE/PAY/CLOSE), the printer may have printed the receipt. The driver reports it as
  `OFFLINE` with `uncertain: true`; the app keeps the receipt `sent` (no error columns: the table CHECK allows an
  error only in `failed`) and writes only the audit `receipt233.uncertain`. While `sent`, the 230 and 157
  invoices of the order are refused ("w trakcie druku"), so the sale cannot get a receipt and an unlinked
  invoice. `OFFLINE` before `OPEN` stays a certain failure (`failed`, "Ponów druk"). No migration.
- Printer I/O happens between database transactions (a slow or offline printer holds no transaction) and
  is serialized per printer in the app process.
- Errors never carry bytes received from the peer (they could be another local service's banner); they
  go only to the server log.
- `server/receipt233-fake-printer.ts` is the local fake: fiscal memory per job, receipt numbers, the daily
  report per fiscal date with VAT per PTU total, and faults (paper out, offline, another PTU table), also
  through the test command `FAULT PAPER_OUT|OFFLINE|NONE`.

## Rules and refusals (Polish, 409 unless noted)
- No active printer; an order not paid yet (`new`), cancelled, with a return, not a sale, merged into
  another, in a currency other than PLN, delivered outside Poland (OSS/export), B2B, already invoiced, with
  a zero total; a line without a PTU letter (NP/OO/OSS or a rate the printer lacks); a second receipt for
  the order. An invalid NIP is 422. A viewer or a read-only key is 403; a read-only copy 423; without 233 503.
- The printer configuration and the connection test of a draft configuration need the admin. A reading role
  can test only the stored printer.
- The same `client_key` replays its own job (200) and prints nothing more; other data with it is 409.

## Invoice to a receipt
- **FV-S (230) accepts it.** Its calculation, preview and issue look up the order's receipt:
  - a receipt still in print blocks the invoice;
  - a business buyer needs its NIP on the receipt, the same NIP (art. 106b ust. 5);
  - the gross must equal the receipt's;
  - the issue writes `receipt233_invoice_links` in its own transaction (same advisory lock as the receipt).
- **157 refuses it.** The pinned 157 preview carries the confirmation "Ta faktura nie jest wystawiana do
  paragonu fiskalnego" (`not_fiscal_receipt_confirmed` must be true in the pinned SQL), so the 157 paths
  (`tax-issue`, the `/issue` alias, Base `addInvoice`) refuse an order with a receipt and point to FV-S.
  The confirmation itself is unchanged; the receipt panel says this next to the receipt.
- **FA(3):** `Fa/FP` = 1 (invoice under art. 109 ust. 3d) after the correction elements, and a
  `DodatkowyOpis` with `Klucz` "Paragon fiskalny" and `Wartosc` "nr … z dnia …, kasa …". FA(3) has no
  dedicated element for the receipt number. Element order follows the existing renderer; the official XSD is
  still not in the repository. A KOR with FP is refused.
- **Print:** the FV-S print shows "Faktura do paragonu fiskalnego nr … z dnia …, kasa …".

## Settlement report and RODO
- Settlement: printed receipts are a sale document. Per order, `receipted`, `receipt_numbers` and
  `receipt_difference` = receipted − net paid. New flags: `paragon_niezgodny` (paid ≠ receipted) and
  `paragon_niewydrukowany` (queued or failed). `brak_faktury` is not raised when a receipt is printed.
  An invoice to the receipt is counted once. CSV columns `paragony_gr`, `paragony`, `roznica_paragonow_gr`.
- RODO: receipts are tax documents with retention from their fiscal date (5 years after the end of the year of
  the VAT due date); the buyer NIP on a receipt is kept, and an order with a receipt keeps its buyer data
  for that period.

## UI
- **Ustawienia → Drukarka fiskalna:** the active printer; the admin form (name, family, loopback host,
  port, unique number, PTU letters A–G, active); "Zapisz konfigurację", "Sprawdź połączenie" (HELLO,
  unique number, PTU table), "Drukuj raport dobowy" and the list of daily reports with their match.
- **Order view → Paragon fiskalny:** lines with PTU letters, PTU totals, refusals; payment form, optional
  NIP (with the simplified-invoice note up to 450 zł), explicit confirmation, "Drukuj paragon"; then the
  number and fiscal date, or the printer error with "Ponów druk"; an unconfirmed print is repeated with the
  same request; the invoice issued to the receipt, or where to issue it.

## Reversible choices (233)
1. The customary PTU table (A 23, B 8, C 5, D 0, E zw; F/G unused) as the default; the letter follows the
   configured table.
2. NP/OO/OSS lines refuse the receipt; EUR orders and deliveries outside Poland are refused.
3. One receipt per order; a failed one is retried, never replaced.
4. The 157 local invoice is refused for an order with a receipt (its pinned confirmation); the invoice to
   a receipt is a FV-S (230).
5. The receipt amount uses the invoice math per line; the daily report compares gross per PTU.
6. A receipt is allowed from `paid` up to `completed`, not for `new`, cancelled or return states.
7. Default payment form: transfer for prepaid orders, "Pobranie u kuriera" for COD.
8. The printer must be on a loopback address; every family uses DYNA-FISC/1 in this version.
