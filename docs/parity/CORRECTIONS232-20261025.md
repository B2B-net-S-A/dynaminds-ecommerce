## What it does
- **Three ways to correct** a FV-S invoice, always from its current state (the invoice, or the state after
  its last correction):
  - line edit: quantity and/or gross value per line, with a reason from the list;
  - from a received return case: the accepted quantity and refund per item, plus the shipping refund;
  - cancellation to zero.
- **Amounts** follow the 230 rule: per line from the gross amount, half up; ZW/NP/OO with VAT 0; OSS at the
  destination rate stored on the line. Categories are in P_13 order with before / after / difference.
- **EUR** uses the NBP rate of the corrected invoice. The PLN difference is PLN(after) − PLN(before), so the
  invoice plus all its corrections give the PLN of the final state to the grosz.
- **Reasons** (Polish `PrzyczynaKorekty`) carry their direction. An increase is accepted only for a cause that
  arose after the sale (art. 29a ust. 17, period of the correction); a decrease is settled in the period of
  the correction (art. 29a ust. 13).
- **Numbering**: `KOR-S/<prefix>/<year>/<sequence>`, an own sub-series of the 157 series of the corrected
  invoice, continuous within itself. The 157 and 230 counters are never touched.
- **Refusals** (nothing is numbered): beyond the invoiced quantity, mixed directions, no change, an increase
  for a decrease reason, a second cancellation, a second correction of the same return, a return not yet
  received, an invoice still waiting for its KSeF number, no permission (viewer, read-only key: 403),
  read-only copy (423), migration missing (503).

## Outputs
- **Routes** `/api/invoice232`:
  - invoice state, calculate, previews, issue (with replay), register, detail, print;
  - `/api/invoice230/documents/:id/corrections` is the KOR-S preview alias.
- **FA(3) KOR**:
  - `RodzajFaktury` KOR, `TypKorekty` 2;
  - `DaneFaKorygowanej` with `NrKSeFFaKorygowanej` (or `NrKSeFN` 1 without a KSeF number);
  - `StanPrzed` rows; signed P_13/P_14/P_15;
  - P_18/P_18A/P_19 of the invoice; EUR `KursWaluty` and P_14_xW from the stored PLN differences.
- **KSeF**: the correction is sent like any document through 229 (`document_kind` `invoice232_correction`).
  It is refused while the corrected invoice waits for its KSeF number.
- **JPK**:
  - JPK_V7: a row in the period of the correction, signed K_ fields in PLN, `NrKSeF`/`OFF`/`BFK`;
  - JPK_FA: `KOREKTA`.
- **Settlement report**: corrections are read from the ledger; a return correction plus its refund give
  "zgodne".
- **RODO**: previews and corrections are kept with tax retention.
- **UI**:
  - under an issued FV-S invoice, the "Korekty (KOR-S)" section lists the corrections (print, FA(3), KSeF
    state and send) and opens the editor with "Wystaw korektę";
  - received returns without a correction get "Koryguj ze zwrotu …", which calculates at once;
  - the documents view has the KOR-S register.

## Reversible choices (232)
1. An own ledger instead of a `documents` row (as 188/206), so none of the core `documents` triggers changes.
2. A KOR-S sub-series per 157 series and year, separate from FV-S and 157 numbering.
3. EUR corrections at the NBP rate of the corrected invoice; PLN difference = PLN(after) − PLN(before).
4. A correction is refused while the corrected invoice waits for its KSeF number (no `NrKSeFN` guess).
5. A return correction takes the accepted quantity and refund per item from the received return; one
   correction per return.
6. After an issue the order view reloads (`onChanged`), which collapses the deferred invoice panel; the
   correction is listed when it is opened again.
