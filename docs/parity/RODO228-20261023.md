# RODO 228 — export, anonymisation and the access register, stage 3 step 3 (23.10.2026)

## What it does
- **Access register.** It records who read or exported a customer's personal data, when, why and through which channel (panel session or API key).
- **Export (art. 15 and 20).** All of a customer's data downloads as one ZIP holding `dane-klienta.json` and `dane-klienta.csv`.
- **Anonymisation (art. 17).** The flow is request → preview → confirm.
  - Invoices still in tax retention keep the buyer data the law requires. Everything else is anonymised.
  - Each kept field is recorded with its legal basis, its retention end and the day from which it is due for anonymisation ("do anonimizacji po terminie").
- **Access.** Everything is administrator only, through a session. An API key is refused with 403.

## Tables (own, migration `228_rodo`)
- **`rodo228_access_log`** (append-only). Columns:
  - tenant, `accessed_at`, actor (user), `api_key_id`, `channel` session|api;
  - `action`: read | list | export | anonymisation_preview | anonymisation;
  - `purpose`, `resource`, `crm_client_id`, `subject_hash`, `order_count`, `detail` (no personal values).
  - `subject_hash` is an HMAC of the normalised e-mail or phone. The key is derived from the installation's `encryptionKey`. The register can therefore be filtered by e-mail or phone without storing them, and the hash cannot be reversed without the key.
  - The guard `rodo228_access_log_guard()` blocks UPDATE and TRUNCATE, also for the owner.
  - DELETE is allowed only inside `rodo228_purge_access_log()` (SECURITY DEFINER), and only for rows older than the tenant's retention.
  - `dyna_app` may read and insert only.
- **`rodo228_settings`.** Register retention per tenant: 180–3650 days, default 1825 (5 years).
- **`rodo228_requests`.** The anonymisation request: subject hash, CRM ids and order ids, the preview `plan` (tables, rows, fields, basis, dates; no personal values), `plan_sha256` and `result`. The guard allows only previewed → completed, once, with the plan unchanged.
- **`rodo228_retained`.** The fields kept by a completed request: table, row, order, fields, basis (`tax_retention` | `immutable_evidence`), legal basis text, `retention_end` and `due_on`. The CHECK `due_on = retention_end + 1` holds. The only update allowed is marking a field done, once.
- **Isolation.**
  - RLS `tenant_scope` applies to all four tables.
  - There is no foreign key to any core table: ids are plain uuids. The only foreign key, `retained→requests`, is between own tables.
  - Core tables get nothing.
  - The compiled cold delta has 182 additions and 0 changes, all with the `rodo228_` prefix.

## Rules (`server/rodo228-model.ts`, pure)
- **Tax point of a document.** It is the sale date (`sell_date`/`sale_date`). The issue date is used instead when it is earlier than the sale date. A document without dates uses the day it was created, in Warsaw time.
- **Tax retention end.** It is `31.12.(Y+5)`, where Y is the year in which the VAT for that tax point was due: the 25th of the next month (art. 112 ustawy o VAT, art. 103 ust. 1, art. 70 § 1 Ordynacji).
  - A December tax point is therefore kept one year longer. Example: 31.12.2026 → 31.12.2032, while 30.11.2026 → 31.12.2031.
  - This deliberately follows the payment deadline rather than the bare year of the tax point. It is never shorter than the law requires.
- **Immutable evidence end.** It is `31.12.(year of the order + 6)` (art. 118 KC, end of a year), but never before the tax end of the same order.
- **Due date.** It is the retention end + 1 day. The last day of the retention still keeps the data.
- **Refusal (409, Polish reason with order and return numbers).** Anonymisation is refused when any of these holds:
  - an order is in any status other than delivered, completed, cancelled or return_completed (a shipped order counts as open);
  - a return is `requested` or `refund_pending`;
  - for any order, `paymentBalance` shows `amount_due > 0`, `overpayment > 0` or `courier_due ≠ 0`.
- **Identifying the customer.**
  - It is exactly one of: CRM id, e-mail (trimmed, lower case) or phone (digits; the last 9 are compared).
  - Orders match by the same e-mail or phone, or by a `crm_order_links` link to a matching CRM profile.
  - Unknown → 404 "Nie znaleziono klienta o podanych danych", or "Nie znaleziono klienta CRM" for a CRM id.

## What is anonymised and what is kept
| Place | Anonymised | Kept (basis) |
|---|---|---|
| Order whose invoice is still in tax retention | e-mail → `anonim-<id8>@anonim.invalid`, `address.phone`, note, personal values inside `source_snapshot` and `b2b_snapshot` | name, street, city, postal code, country (tax_retention, until the invoice's retention end) |
| Order without an invoice, or whose retention has ended | also name ("Klient zanonimizowany"), street, city ("Zanonimizowano") and postal code (00-000) | — |
| Invoices and corrections (`documents`, `return_credit206_documents`, `local_consumer_credit_documents`) with their preview/receipt/admission records, FA(3) drafts, the FV-S records of 230 (`invoice230_previews`, `invoice230_documents`: buyer name, address, NIP / EU VAT ID), the KSeF files of these documents (`ksef229_submissions`: FA(3) XML and UPO), and `invoice_plans` of a retained order | — | everything (tax_retention until the invoice's retention end; immutable_evidence once the retention has ended) |
| CRM profile | login, phone, e-mail, notes, invoice and delivery data | star, contractor id, status |
| CRM extra values (consents "Zgody", text and other fields, file links) | deleted | — |
| CRM attachments (`crm_files`), CRM command journal (`crm_commands`) | — | immutable_evidence. The app role cannot change them; the replay check hashes the journal. The attachment is no longer reachable from the card once its value is gone. |
| Shipping/invoice plans, channel imports (`inbound_orders`), shipments' tracking data, order events, return notes/request/receipt | known values of the customer replaced with `[zanonimizowano]` | — |
| API replays (`idempotency_keys`), channel webhooks, `audit_events` | the same scrub, on rows found by the customer's e-mail (unique) within the tenant | — |
| Immutable evidence of an order: changed delivery address (encrypted), receipts, delivery evidence, merge origins, WMS operations, Shopify dispatch, return admissions, WZ admissions, return assistant sessions, portal messages, sent e-mails and SMS, and payment entries whose note, reference or request contains a personal value | — | immutable_evidence |
| Any row whose guard refuses the UPDATE (e.g. "Przydział paczek zamraża odbiorcę i zamówienie", merged source orders) | — | immutable_evidence, with the guard's message as the reason |

**How changes are applied.**
- Every change is an ordinary `UPDATE` (plus the DELETE of `crm_values`) run as `dyna_app` in the application transaction.
- Each update runs in its own savepoint. When a guard trigger (PL/pgSQL; codes P0001, 55000, 23514, 22023) or a missing grant (42501) refuses, the row is rolled back to the savepoint and recorded as kept.
- Any other error aborts the whole operation.
- No trigger, column, constraint or grant was added to a core table.

**Preview and confirmation.**
- The preview runs the same function inside a transaction that is then rolled back. The plan therefore shows exactly what the confirmation will do.
- After the rollback, the request row and a register row are written.
- The confirmation runs again on the stored ids and compares the plan hash. If the data changed, it returns 409 "Dane klienta zmieniły się od podglądu".
- It then writes `rodo228_retained`, completes the request and writes the register row, all in one transaction.
- Confirming a completed request again returns the stored result (`replayed: true`).
- A new preview of an already anonymised customer finds nothing more to change in the orders or the CRM.

## Register hooks
- **Customer card (`server/crm-routes.ts`).** The optional `access` callback is installed by buildApp. One row per customer, after the read and before the reply, for:
  - `GET /api/crm/clients` (list),
  - `/clients/:id`,
  - `/clients/:id/orders`,
  - `/clients/:id/files/:fieldId`.
  - Rows that carry only a CRM id still get the hash of the profile's e-mail or phone.
- **Base CRM `connector.php`.** `observeBaseWireReads()` in `server/base-wire-routes.ts` is a module-level observer, installed only by buildApp.
  - It covers getCrmClientData, getCrmClients and the attachment download.
  - It records channel `api` with the API key and the local CRM id taken from `base_wire218_client_ids`.
- **When nothing is written.** Without 228, or in a read-only copy, nothing is written. A read whose register row cannot be written is not returned.
- **How the hook rows are written.** Both hooks use `tenantAppendTx` (`server/db.ts`). It is one short read-write transaction as `dyna_app` in the tenant, inside the ordinary schema lease when that runtime is installed. It runs none of `tenantTx`'s domain admissions or preflights, so its callers may only INSERT into their own append-only tables.
  - The reason: without the ordinary runtime (a plain `buildApp`, as in the browser check), every `tenantTx` pays about 10 s of installation preflights (measured on clones 01 and 04: `tenantTx` 10085–10195 ms, `tenantAppendTx` 1 ms).
  - With `tenantTx`, the CRM list, which the UI aborts after 15 s, stopped loading.
  - Export and anonymisation keep `tenantTx`.
- **Hash-pinned files.** `local-invoices.ts`, `local-invoice-routes.ts` and `local-invoice-values.ts` are unchanged.

## API (session, administrator)
- `GET /api/rodo228/status`
- `GET /api/rodo228/export?crm_client_id|email|phone[&purpose]` → `application/zip`, `Cache-Control: private, no-store`. In a read-only copy it returns 423, because the register cannot be written.
- `POST /api/rodo228/anonymisation/preview {crm_client_id|email|phone}` → `{request_id, plan_sha256, plan}`, or 404/409.
- `POST /api/rodo228/anonymisation/:id/confirm {plan_sha256}`
- `GET /api/rodo228/access-log?email|phone|crm_client_id|action|actor_id|from|to|limit|before`: newest first, cursor `before`, with actor and API-key names.
- `POST /api/rodo228/access-log/purge`
- `PUT /api/rodo228/settings {access_log_retention_days}`
- `GET /api/rodo228/requests`
- `GET /api/rodo228/retained?due=1|0`: pending kept fields; `due=1` lists only those whose due date has passed.
- `rodo228_available` in `/api/health`.

## Reversible choices made
- **Shipped counts as open.** Delivery is not yet confirmed and COD may still be open.
- **The December retention rule follows the VAT payment deadline.** It never ends before the bare year-of-tax-point rule.
- **Immutable evidence is kept for 6 years** under art. 118 KC, at the end of the year.
- **E-mail and phone in the register are keyed hashes,** never plain values.
- **The CRM profile stays** with its personal fields emptied, not soft-deleted, so the card still shows that the customer was anonymised. The order links stay.
- **On an invoiced order, e-mail and phone are anonymised.** The invoice itself still holds the e-mail.
- **The export is one ZIP holding JSON and CSV.**
  - The CSV is long-form (sekcja, rekord, pole, wartość), with a BOM and `;` separators. Formula cells get a leading apostrophe.
  - CRM attachments are described by metadata only.
- **The preview refuses with 409 for open business** instead of showing a blocked plan.
- **Tenant-wide JSON (API replays, webhooks, audit) is matched by e-mail only,** not by name, so another person with the same name is not touched.

## Open for later steps
- **Completing kept fields after their due date** needs a worker or a manual action. The list exists; marking items done is possible in SQL (the guard allows it once), but there is no route yet.
- **Immutable evidence can only be completed by a controlled procedure,** because its own guards block the application. This covers the buyer-address sidecar, receipts, `crm_commands`, `crm_files`, sent e-mail and SMS, portal messages and payment notes.
- **Order merge previews and executions** hold order snapshots without an `order_id` column. They are not yet listed in the plan.
- **The unsalted `buyer_basis_hash`** (166/209) can be re-identified by guessing. It stays, because it is part of immutable receipts.
- **Not covered:**
  - CRM file contents in the export;
  - B2B accounts and contact persons (0 rows today);
  - a subject matched by name alone.

## FV-S invoices and KSeF files (24.10.2026)
- **What the plan keeps.** It keeps the 230 records of the customer's orders and the 229 KSeF submissions of the orders' documents:
  - `invoice230_previews`: snapshot.buyer, request;
  - `invoice230_documents`: buyer_tax_id, snapshot.buyer (key `document_id`);
  - `ksef229_submissions`: xml, upo_xml (by `document_id`).

  Each item is kept with basis `tax_retention`, the legal basis text and the retention end of its own invoice. `invoice230_categories` holds no personal data and is not listed.
- **No 228 migration.** `source_table` is text, and the `tax_retention` basis already exists.
- **Export.** The ZIP already lists FV-S invoices, with the buyer and NIP / EU VAT ID, because they are `documents` rows.
- **Labels in the preview.** "Podgląd faktury FV-S", "Faktura FV-S (B2B, EUR, ZW/NP/OO/OSS)", "Plik faktury wysłany do KSeF"; fields "NIP / numer VAT UE nabywcy", "dane nabywcy", "plik FA(3)", "UPO".
- **Effect on stored previews.** A preview made before this change gets a different plan hash on confirmation, so it answers 409 "Dane klienta zmieniły się od podglądu". The fix is to prepare the preview again.
- **Verified.** `tests/gaps231.database.test.ts`: export, preview and anonymisation of a customer with a FV-S invoice sent to KSeF. The FV-S record stays unchanged, `rodo228_retained` has the three rows with `tax_retention`, and the guards are true. The browser preview on clone `_03` lists all three; the screenshot is in the scratchpad folder `gaps231-shots/`.
