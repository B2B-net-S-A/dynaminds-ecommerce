# Invoices 230 — ZW/NP/OO/OSS, B2B buyer, MPP in PLN, EUR (stage 3 step 5, 23.10.2026)

## What it does
The 157 local invoice covers one case: a PL consumer, PLN, ordinary rates 0/5/8/23. Migration 230 adds an issue path for everything else this step needs:
- a B2B buyer: NIP for PL, an EU VAT ID for the EU, no tax id outside the EU;
- lines exempt from VAT (ZW), outside the scope of Polish VAT (NP I, NP II) and under reverse charge (OO);
- OSS sales to EU consumers;
- invoices in EUR, with the NBP rate stored at issue.

It also adds the MPP marking. The 157 SQL and the pinned `local-invoices.ts`, `local-invoice-routes.ts` and `local-invoice-values.ts` are unchanged.

## Numbering (decision of the coordinator, logged for the owner, reversible)
- A 230 invoice is numbered **FV-S/&lt;prefix&gt;/&lt;year&gt;/&lt;sequence&gt;**. This is its own sub-series of a 157 series definition, the same pattern as 159 B2B (`FV-B2B/…`).
- The sub-series is continuous within itself. The counter is `invoice230_counters`, per series and year. It starts at 1 and moves by exactly one, and only together with a document of that number (deferred check). It is never deleted.
- Why there is no continuation of the 157 sequence:
  - The 157 counter moves only with a 157 receipt (`server/157_local_invoices.sql:256–264`).
  - A 157 receipt needs a preview equal to `local_invoice_frame`, and the frame refuses EUR and non-consumer buyers (`:157–160`).
  - A shared number would therefore block the next 157 issue on the unique index `document_demo_number`.
- The 157 sequence is unchanged. The DB test issues a 157 invoice before and after seven 230 invoices: they get `…/000001` and `…/000002`, and the 230 invoices get `FV-S/…/000001…000007`.
- One invoice per order across both paths:
  - A 230 issue refuses an order that already has any non-demo invoice.
  - The 157 `tax-issue`, Base `addInvoice`, the external (Fakturownia) plans, the B2B FA(3) projects and the Odoo export refuse an order with a 230 invoice (`assertNoInvoice230InTx`).
  - The pinned native `POST /api/local-invoices/previews/:id/issue` is covered since 231 (24.10.2026): an app-level guard in `server/app.ts` refuses an order with a FV-S or another 157 invoice (409) and answers every other request with the `tax-issue` handler, so `/issue` is an alias of `tax-issue` (see "Gaps closed by 231").

## Tables (own, migration `230_invoices`)
- **`invoice230_previews`** — what the user saw and confirmed: the order version, the 157 series, the request, and the full snapshot.
  - `scope_hash` = sha256 of the `jsonb` text (CHECKed).
  - The preview expires after 15 minutes.
- **`invoice230_counters`** — the sub-series counter (see above).
- **`invoice230_documents`** — the issued invoice. It has the only foreign key to a core table (`documents`) and points to its preview. It stores:
  - number, sequence, buyer kind/country/tax-id kind/tax id, PL VAT payer status;
  - currency, procedure (domestic/oss), net/VAT/gross, annex-15 gross;
  - MPP, reverse charge, and the exemption kind + basis;
  - for EUR: tax point and its basis, NBP table date and number, mid, source, and the PLN totals;
  - `snapshot` = `documents.data`.

  CHECKs encode the rules: PLN ⇔ no rate, EUR ⇔ full rate; OSS only PLN and consumer; MPP only B2B with annex 15 over 15 000 zł (in PLN); NIP only for PL; an EU VAT ID only outside PL; the number format. A BEFORE INSERT guard requires all of these to agree: the documents row (kind, mode `invoice230`, order, number, data), the preview (order, series, actor, key, scope, snapshot) and the counter value.
- **`invoice230_categories`** — net/VAT per FA(3) category: `23`, `8`, `5`, `0 KR`, `zw`, `np I`, `np II`, `oo`, and `oss <rate>`. For EUR each row also has the PLN amounts. CHECK: `net_pln = round(net × mid)`, `vat_pln = round(vat × mid)`, `gross_pln = net_pln + vat_pln`. A foreign key on `(tenant, document, mid)` to the header forces the header's mid. A deferred check at commit makes the categories sum to the header.
- Previews, documents and categories are append-only, and TRUNCATE is refused on all four tables, also for the owner.
- RLS `tenant_scope`. `dyna_app` gets SELECT and INSERT, plus UPDATE(`last_number`) on the counter.
- Nothing is attached to core tables.
- 227 keeps its CHECK `vat_rate IN (0,5,8,23)`, so ZW/NP/OO/OSS PLN amounts live only in 230. For an EUR invoice whose categories are all 227 VAT rates, the 227 record (`nbp227_document_rates/_vat`) is also written in the issuing transaction.

## Rules (pure, `server/invoice230-model.ts`)
- **Buyer.**
  - Consumer.
  - Business with a NIP (10-digit checksum; `PL` and dashes are accepted).
  - Business with an EU VAT ID: country prefix, `EL` for Greece, VIES national format, no VIES call. It is optional, but NP II needs it.
  - Business outside the EU: no tax id.
  - "Czynny podatnik VAT w Polsce" is the user's confirmation, PL only. OO needs it.
  - A consumer's country must be the delivery country of the order.
- **Line categories.** These come from the 226 product profiles (`deriveLineTax`). The buyer given to those rules now carries `eu_vat_id` and `pl_vat_payer` (fix of `tax226.ts:94`).
  - Shipping follows the goods when every item line has the same special category.
  - Otherwise shipping is an ordinary line at the chosen shipping rate, or at the OSS standard rate for an EU consumer.
  - Free shipping that cannot be documented abroad is left off.
- **Rounding = 157.** Per line, from the gross amount: VAT = gross × r / (100 + r), rounded half up to a grosz/cent, and net = gross − VAT (`gross_line_half_up`). Two-decimal OSS rates are handled the same way. ZW, NP I, NP II and OO have net = gross and VAT 0; 0% is `0 KR`. Category totals are sums of lines, in P_13 order.
- **MPP.** B2B, at least one annex-15 line, and the total due strictly over 15 000,00 zł. A EUR total is compared after the 227 conversion (per category, half up). Exactly 15 000,00 zł is not MPP.
- **EUR.**
  - The rate is the stored 227 rate of the tax point (the sell date): the last table before it with proven coverage. No network call is made at issue.
  - PLN amounts are computed per category.
  - OSS in EUR is refused for now (the OSS return converts with the ECB rate).
- **Refusals** (Polish, 409 at preview and issue):
  - ZW without a basis (also 422 on the product profile);
  - NP II without an EU VAT ID or to a consumer;
  - NP I to a PL buyer;
  - OO to a non-payer;
  - OSS without registration on the sell date or without the destination rate;
  - EUR without a stored rate;
  - a foreign business buyer with taxable goods (WDT);
  - different exemption bases on one invoice;
  - an order that is not a shipped sale, or that has a return, correction, merge, external invoice, B2B project or partial commercial scope;
  - a second invoice for the same order.
- **Errors without issuing.** No permission → 403 (viewer, a key without `documents:write`). Invalid NIP or VAT ID → 422. Read-only copy → 423.

## API
- `POST /api/invoice230/orders/:id/calculate` — reading role. Returns the snapshot the issue would store now and the blockers (no write).
- `POST /api/invoice230/orders/:id/previews` — admin/operator. Stores the preview and returns `issue_path`.
- `POST /api/local-invoices/previews/:id/tax-issue` — the existing route. When the id is a 230 preview it issues in the sub-series, in one transaction:
  - native identity frame and invoice lock (shared with 157);
  - recompute the snapshot (it must equal the preview: the order, profiles, OSS and rate are unchanged);
  - counter, documents row, header and categories;
  - for EUR, the 227 record when it can hold it;
  - audit, then `SET CONSTRAINTS ALL IMMEDIATE`.

  The same `client_key` replays (200).
- `GET /api/invoice230/previews/:id`, `GET /api/invoice230/documents[?order_id]`, `GET /api/invoice230/documents/:id` — the record must equal `documents.data` (503 otherwise).
- `GET /api/invoice230/documents/:id/print`. `/api/documents/:id/print` dispatches mode `invoice230` there.
- `invoice230_available` is reported in `/api/health`.

## Print, FA(3), JPK, settlement
- **Print** (`server/invoice230-print.ts`):
  - buyer NIP or "Numer VAT UE";
  - the rate label of every line (23%, zw, np, oo, 0%, 19% (OSS));
  - a summary per category, with "VAT w PLN" for EUR;
  - the marks: "mechanizm podzielonej płatności", "odwrotne obciążenie", the art. 28b note for NP II, "Zwolnienie z VAT: <basis>" and "Procedura szczególna OSS";
  - for EUR, the line "Kurs NBP z dnia …, tabela …, 1 EUR = … PLN; VAT w PLN: … zł" and the PLN totals.
- **FA(3)** (`server/invoice230-fa3.ts` through the single entry point `GET /api/fa3/documents/:id/fa3.xml`):
  - `Podmiot2` has `NIP`, `KodUE` + `NrVatUE` (new in `fa3-render.ts`; the number without its prefix) or `BrakID`;
  - `P_12` per line; OSS uses `P_12_XII`;
  - `P_13_x`/`P_14_x`, `P_18=1` for oo/np II, `P_18A=1` for MPP, `P_19` with `P_19A/B/C`;
  - EUR: `KodWaluty`, `KursWaluty` on every line and `P_14_xW` from the stored PLN.
- **JPK_V7** (`jpk-source.ts`: mode `invoice230`):
  - K_ fields as before: `zw` K_10, `np I` K_11, `np II` K_11 + K_12, `oss` K_11 + `WSTO_EE`, `0 KR` K_13, 5/8/23 K_15–K_20, `oo` K_31, `MPP`;
  - EUR in PLN;
  - an EU buyer gets `KodKrajuNadaniaTIN` (ISO, GR for Greece) + `NrKontrahenta` (the VAT number without prefix);
  - JPK_FA puts `P_5A`/`P_5B` from the VAT ID.
- **Settlement report.** It reads `totals.gross_minor` of mode `invoice230`.
- **KSeF.** Since 231 a FV-S invoice is sent to KSeF like the other documents (online, offline, queue; number and UPO on the 229 submission; JPK `NrKSeF`). Without 231 the send answers 409 and the FA(3) XML can be downloaded.

## Reversible choices made in this step
1. Sub-series `FV-S/<prefix>/<year>/<seq>` per 157 series (coordinator decision).
2. Shipping follows special-category goods. Free shipping that cannot be documented abroad is left off the invoice.
3. OSS only in PLN.
4. The EU VAT ID is optional for an EU business on the server and required in the UI form for the "Firma z UE" type. NP II always needs it.
5. The "czynny podatnik VAT" status is the user's confirmation (no White List / Biała lista call).
6. A consumer's country must equal the delivery country.
7. The 227 record is written only when every category is a 227 VAT rate. Otherwise the PLN amounts are only in 230.
8. A 230 invoice is refused for orders with a return or correction, as 157 does.
9. Demo/simulation/provider_test invoices do not block a 230 invoice.
10. Corrections of 230 invoices are refused with 409. (KSeF sending was refused too until 231.)

## Not done / open

- The official FA(3)/JPK XSD is still not in the repository (as before), so KodUE/NrVatUE and KodKrajuNadaniaTIN are checked only against the structure already encoded.
